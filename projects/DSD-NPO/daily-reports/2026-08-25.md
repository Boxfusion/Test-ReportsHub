# QA Report — Tuesday 2026-08-25

**Module:** DSD-NPO (Department of Social Development — NPO Registration & Compliance Portal) — QA environment

**Headline:** 7 new suites authored and executed the same day, and **three long-standing blockers were cleared** — the appeals journey, the audit trail, and the sign-up inventory. **55 scripts run, 47 of them for the first time** · overall functional progress **51% → 61%**.

## 1. Module progress

| Module | New scripts | Re-run | Total run | Result | Overall progress |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **DSD-NPO** | **47** | 8 | 55 | 5 pass · 17 fail · 12 partial · 10 blocked · 11 not executed | **193 of 314 (61%)** |

*"New scripts" = test scenarios authored and driven for the first time. Seven suites were newly authored today (06, 09, 14C, 14N, 14U, 14X, 14Y); a further two were unblocked and driven from plans that already existed (11A, 11P). The 8 re-runs are the 14T/14D close-out, less TC-06 — that case was deliberately left un-run on 08-24, so it counts as new.*

**How overall progress is measured.** 193 of the 314 cases in ADO Functional Test Plan **101543** now carry a real black-box verdict — pass, fail or partial. That is 61.5%; **61% is the conservative figure and the one to quote.** Blocked and not-executed cases are excluded from the numerator and stay in the denominator. The number comes from `scripts/coverage-baseline.js`, which de-duplicates across reports on the ADO case id, so it is reproducible rather than carried forward.

Run over the same report set with today's runs excluded, the script reads **161 of 314 (51%)** — that is the like-for-like starting point. Today's 55 in-scope runs produced 34 verdicts, of which **32 were new to the count**; the other two were re-verdicts of certificate cases already counted — the certificate QR pair, re-issued in `test-reports/2026-08-25/14t-14d-close-out--qr-decoded-and-ui-crosscheck.md`.

⚠️ **This does not match the 154 (49%) published on 08-24, and the difference is not new testing.** Two 18 August reports had recorded their verdicts in vocabulary the parser does not recognise (`CONFIRMED` / `NOT CONFIRMABLE`), so those cases were counted as *assumed* rather than verdicted. Normalising that vocabulary today lifted the pre-08-25 baseline from 154 to 161. The gain attributable to today's testing is the **+32**, not +39.

Cumulative split across all 193: **62 pass · 83 fail · 48 partial**.
